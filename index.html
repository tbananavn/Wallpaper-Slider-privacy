Privacy Policy

App: Wallpaper Slider  ·  Last updated: June 23, 2026

In short: This app works fully offline and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Wallpaper Slider ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. We built this app to function entirely on your device, without an internet connection, and without collecting any information about you.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically, the app does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or other contact details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics data
  • Photos, contacts, files, or any other content stored on your device, unless you explicitly choose to use a feature that accesses them, in which case that data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere

Because the app operates offline, it has no server to send data to, and no account or sign-in is required to use it.

3. Permissions

If the app requests any device permissions (for example, storage access to save or open a file), these permissions are used only to provide that specific feature on your device. No data accessed through these permissions is collected, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party.

[If your app requests no permissions at all, you can replace this section with: "This app does not request any device permissions."]

4. Third-Party Services, Ads & Purchases

This app does not contain advertisements, does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. No data is shared with advertising networks or any other third party, because none are integrated into the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Since the app does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the relevant age in your jurisdiction).

6. Data Security

Any data the app uses (such as files you open or settings you change) is stored only locally on your device and is never transmitted elsewhere. You control this data and can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality changes in the future. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
